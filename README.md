# Max/MSP Patch Archive

Recovered Max/MSP patches preserved from Google Drive and historical correspondence.

## Contents

- `oliver-schneller-2019/`: course materials sent by Oliver Schneller (2019), organized by topic.
- `other-2024/`: additional patches found on Drive (2024); authorship not verified.

## Provenance and usage

This is an archival collection. Files are preserved as found, without claiming authorship or a license. The materials attributed to Oliver Schneller are educational files circulated with his composition class in 2019. **No license for redistribution or reuse has been verified.** Verify permission before redistributing third-party material.

The original files are also archived in Google Drive. Some additional mail attachments could not be retrieved through the available Gmail integration and are not included yet.
